## Introduction

Loyalty platforms can integrate their services with any online channel connected to Deliverect by providing a standardized set of webhooks below. These enable seamless loyalty experiences to be embedded using key functions of customer identification, program discovery, wallet balance retrieval, and reward redemption, whilst allowing loyalty providers to maintain full control over their loyalty logic and business rules.
