## Introduction

Customers build menus on Deliverect based on the product Sync data received from their POS systems and publish those menus to the stores. Each store can have one or more menus associated with it.&#x20;

## Publishing Menu Data

When a customer chooses to update their menu on their channels, they will **publish** it, which involves choosing the menu(s) and which channel(s) they should apply to
